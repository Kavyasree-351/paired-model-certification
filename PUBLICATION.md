# Publication scope

This repository publishes the paired-model certification feasibility project and its selective/staged label-acquisition follow-ups. It preserves the original research outcomes, including failed hypotheses and corrected historical attempts. Other ANN/routing projects from the workspace are not included.

The implementation is a reproducible research prototype. No new-method advantage, peer-reviewed publication, conference acceptance or deployment safety beyond the stated statistical assumptions is claimed. `PAPER_DRAFT.md` is explicitly an unfinished negative-study draft, not a published paper.

Publication preparation removes local machine paths from two historical metadata files, excludes bytecode/font caches and the original delivery checksum list, and adds repository documentation and bounded-check entry points. Numerical experiment outcomes are unchanged. Frozen hashes inside historical records refer to original experiment snapshots; `PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json` records this publication copy instead.

`PUBLICATION_TESTS.json` records the checks rerun for publication. Existing experiment records document earlier full pilot/confirmation runs; publishing does not rerun or unblind final evaluation pools. No raw datasets, dependency installations, credentials or local environments are included.
